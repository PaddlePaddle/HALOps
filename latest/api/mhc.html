MHC¶

megallmhc

Fused mHC post + pre + RMSNorm + W8A8-MXFP8 quantization for decode.

For every token: residual_out[j] = bf16(post[j] * (x + shared) + sum_i comb[i, j] * residual[i]); the mix fn @ residual_out with its RMS gives pre, post_out (sigmoid) and comb_out (sinkhorn); layer_out = RMSNorm(bf16(sum_j pre[j] * residual_out[j])) is quantized like group_wise_quant(layer_out, "w8a8-mxfp8", use_swizzle), bit for bit. Hidden size 4096 and 4 hyper-connection streams.

Parameters:

  • x (torch.Tensor) – [T, 4096] bfloat16 layer output.

  • residual (torch.Tensor) – [T, 4, 4096] bfloat16 hyper-connection streams.

  • post (torch.Tensor) – [T, 4] float32 post weights of the previous junction.

  • comb (torch.Tensor) – [T, 4, 4] float32 comb of the previous junction, [source, dest].

  • fn (torch.Tensor) – [24, 16384] float32 mix weights, used at tf32 precision.

  • hc_scale (torch.Tensor) – [3] float32 mix scales.

  • hc_base (torch.Tensor) – [24] float32 mix biases.

  • norm_weight (torch.Tensor) – [4096] bfloat16 RMSNorm weight.

  • rms_eps (float) – Epsilon of the mix RMS.

  • hc_pre_eps (float) – Added to pre.

  • hc_sinkhorn_eps (float) – Sinkhorn epsilon.

  • hc_post_mult_value (float) – Scale of post_out.

  • sinkhorn_repeat (int) – Sinkhorn iterations.

  • norm_eps (float) – Epsilon of the RMSNorm.

  • mhc_align_fleet (bool) – Use 1 / (sqrt(ms) + eps) for the mix RMS and exp2 in sinkhorn, as fleet; off by default.

  • shared (torch.Tensor | None) – Optional [T, 4096] bfloat16 term added to x.

  • use_pdl (bool) – Enable Programmatic Dependent Launch (on by default).

  • use_swizzle (bool) – Scales in the CUTLASS SfAtom layout, rows padded to a multiple of 128.

  • token_tiles (int) – Max token tiles of the first kernel’s grid; does not change the result.

Returns:

(residual_out, layer_out, post_out, comb_out, q, sf) – bfloat16 [T, 4, 4096] and [T, 4096], float32 [T, 4] and [T, 4, 4], float8_e4m3fn [T, 4096] codes and uint8 [T, 128] UE8M0 scales ([ceil(T / 128) * 128, 128] with use_swizzle).

Return type:

Tuple[torch.Tensor, …]
